# Skill canvas: Frame Intent

## Who needs this?

A human and their agents starting a product feature, AI use case, internal
capability, or operating change before a spec-driven workflow has a clear purpose.

## What changes for them?

The next workflow receives an owned outcome, explicit assumptions, and early
indicators tied to decisions. The human can steer on evidence and learn why
those choices matter through the work itself.

## What assumption am I making?

A concise intent brief in the native workflow can improve specification without
imposing another framework. Timely coaching can support human outcome thinking
without slowing delivery; this benefit needs observation, not a claim based on
the presence of teaching text.
