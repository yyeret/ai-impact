# Agility AI Transformation

## Outcome
Translate AI + agility ideas into business-ready narratives, decisions, and commercial assets, while proactively identifying AI-driven workflow or product improvements.

## Outcome Indicators

- Client or stakeholder can articulate the difference between AI theater and outcome-led AI adoption after the session.
- At least one concrete "AI Opportunity" is identified and named per engagement review.
- AI strategy POV is reused in follow-on proposals, outreach, or content without requiring regeneration.

## Discovery Question

I need to help a client (or myself) move from "we're experimenting with AI" to a credible, outcome-first AI transformation narrative — how do I build that without falling into AI theater?

## Gotchas

- **Start with the expensive problem, not the AI tool.** Leading with LLMs or automation technology before naming the business problem is the exact AI theater pattern this skill is designed to counter.
- **"Proactive background daemon" mode is active by default.** This skill's AI Opportunity scan should run in the background of every client engagement — not just when explicitly invoked.
- **Distinguish BU-level improvements from enterprise-level strategic impact.** Conflating tactical workflow improvements with enterprise transformation produces overclaiming that loses credibility with senior stakeholders.

## Proactive AI Mandate
This skill operates in the background of every engagement. Its mandate is to:
- **Rule:** Proactively look for AI-driven improvements to client workflows, products, or strategic positioning, even if not explicitly requested.
- **Goal:** Infuse AI-first strategy into every delivery task to maximize client value and Yuval's strategic impact.

## Workflow
1. **Background Audit:** Continuously monitor engagement activities for AI-driven leverage points.
2. **Context Discovery:** Define the expensive business problem and affected stakeholder.
3. **Contrast Strategy:** Contrast "AI theater" patterns with outcome-led, AI-infused operating models.
4. **POV Development:** Build executive POVs with proof anchors and implementation constraints.
5. **Offer Packaging:** Package AI transformation offers or messaging options by buyer segment.

## Rules
- **Start with outcomes, not tools.**
- **Proactive Search:** Always ask: "How could AI fundamentally improve this workflow or product?"
- **Distinguish Impact:** Separate BU-level improvements from enterprise-level strategic impact.
- **Decision-Oriented:** Keep language operator-facing and focused on immediate strategic decisions.

## Output Schemas
- `diagnosis`: Problem -> Evidence -> Decision -> Next Action
- `asset`: Audience -> Hook -> Core Claim -> Proof -> CTA
- `ai-opportunity`: Workflow -> Friction Point -> AI Lever -> Business Outcome

## Quality Gates
- Output must name the explicit audience and expensive problem.
- Output must include at least one "AI Opportunity" that identifies a specific leverage point.
- Mark all claims as confirmed evidence or inference explicitly.
